PDF Tools

Merge, split, sign, protect and edit PDFs. 63 free tools that run 100% in your browser.

  • Merge PDF: Combine multiple PDFs into one ordered document with drag-and-drop sorting.
  • Split PDF: Split by ranges, every N pages, or extract each page into a ZIP.
  • Compress PDF: Shrink PDF size with smart image downsampling and stream compression.
  • Organize PDF: Visually reorder, rotate, duplicate and delete pages with thumbnails.
  • Delete Pages: Remove selected pages and download the trimmed document.
  • Extract Pages: Pull selected pages into a brand-new PDF.
  • Rotate PDF: Rotate selected pages or the whole document.
  • Add Page Numbers: Stamp page numbers with position, format and font controls.
  • Watermark PDF: Stamp text or logo watermarks with opacity, rotation and tiling.
  • Header & Footer: Add running headers and footers with dates, titles and page tokens.
  • Sign PDF: Draw, type or upload a signature and place it anywhere, plus dates and initials.
  • Edit PDF: Add text, shapes, highlights, images and freehand drawing to any page.
  • Protect PDF: Encrypt a PDF with a password and set print/copy permissions.
  • Unlock PDF: Remove the password from a PDF you have access to.
  • Redact PDF: Black out areas or auto-find emails, phones and card numbers, then flatten.
  • PDF to Grayscale: Convert color PDFs to grayscale or high-contrast black & white.
  • Crop PDF: Trim page margins visually or by exact measurements.
  • Flatten PDF: Flatten form fields and annotations into static page content.
  • PDF Metadata: View and edit title, author, subject, keywords and dates.
  • Fill PDF Form: Detect form fields and fill text boxes, checkboxes and dropdowns.
  • Compare PDF: Side-by-side text diff of two PDF versions.
  • N-up / Booklet: Print 2, 4 or 9 pages per sheet, or impose a booklet.
  • OCR PDF: Recognise text in scanned PDFs and images in 10+ languages.
  • PDF Reader: Fast viewer with search, zoom, thumbnails and outline.
  • Insert Pages: Insert blank pages or pages from another PDF at any position.
  • Repair PDF: Rebuild broken or malformed PDFs by re-writing the document structure.
  • Invoice Generator: Create GST/VAT invoices with automatic totals and a polished PDF.
  • Resume Builder: Fill a form, pick a template, download an ATS-friendly PDF resume.
  • Change PDF Page Size: Scale or fit pages to A4, Letter, A5 or a custom size, with margins. Text stays sharp.
  • Split Pages in Half: Cut two-page spreads and two-up scans into single pages, left and right or top and bottom.
  • Flip / Mirror PDF: Mirror PDF pages left to right or top to bottom for transfers, back-lit prints and glass scans.
  • Overlay PDF: Lay one PDF over or under another: letterheads, stationery, form backgrounds and stamps.
  • Print Poster (Tile PDF): Enlarge one page across several sheets with overlap, cut marks and sheet labels.
  • Alternate & Mix PDFs: Interleave front and back scans into one double-sided PDF, or mix pages from several files.
  • Edit Bookmarks / Outline: Add, edit, reorder and nest PDF bookmarks, or build them from the headings in the text.
  • Edit PDF Links: Add, move and remove web and page links, or turn every web address in the text into a link.
  • Create Fillable PDF Form: Add text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdowns, lists and signature fields to any PDF.
  • PDF Attachments: List, extract, add and remove files embedded inside a PDF.
  • PDF Viewer Preferences: Set the opening page, zoom, page layout, side panel, full screen and toolbar options.
  • Remove Annotations: Strip comments, highlights, drawings, stamps, links or form fields, by type and page.
  • Sanitize PDF: Remove JavaScript, embedded files, links, metadata, comments and hidden layers before sharing.
  • PDF Inspector: See page sizes, fonts and whether they are embedded, images, scripts, version, encryption and word counts.
  • Rasterize PDF: Turn every page into an image-only PDF at the resolution you choose.
  • Remove Blank Pages: Find and delete blank or nearly blank scanned pages automatically, with a manual override.
  • Adjust PDF Colors: Dark mode, invert, grayscale, sepia, black and white, contrast and color replacement for PDFs.
  • Extract PDF Images: Pull embedded images out of a PDF at original quality and download them as a ZIP.
  • Search in PDFs: Full-text search across many PDFs at once, with context and the match highlighted on the page.
  • Web-Optimize PDF: Linearize a PDF for fast web view and pack it with compressed object streams.
  • PDF to PDF/A: Convert to archival PDF/A-1b, 2b or 3b: color profile, XMP metadata, no scripts, with a strict image mode.
  • Check PDF/A: Quick PDF/A report: identification, fonts, color profile, encryption, scripts, transparency and metadata.
  • PDF Accessibility Checker: Check tags, alt text, language, title, headings, table headers, tab order and real text.

  • PDF Embed Code: Generate HTML to embed a PDF on your website: a PDF.js viewer that works on phones, or iframe and object code.
