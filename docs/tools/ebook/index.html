Tools

  • E-book Converter: Convert EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, CBZ, DOCX, PDF, TXT and more to EPUB, PDF, Word, HTML, TXT, Markdown or FB2.
  • E-book Reader: Read EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2 and CBZ comics in your browser with themes, search and a table of contents.
  • EPUB Editor: Change the cover, title, author, series and tags of an EPUB, shrink its images, or merge several books.
  • Comic Converter: Convert CBZ, CBR, CB7 and PDF comics to PDF, CBZ, CB7 or images, and shrink big comics.
  • EPUB Checker: Check an EPUB for the errors that make stores reject it: mimetype, metadata, manifest, spine, navigation and broken links.
  • Kindle Highlights Exporter: Turn My Clippings.txt into clean Markdown, CSV or JSON, sorted by book, with duplicates merged.
  • eBook Word Count & Stats: Word count, reading time, chapter lengths, vocabulary and reading level for EPUB, MOBI, DOCX or PDF books.
  • eBook Cover Maker: Design a clean book cover with title, author and a photo, at Kindle, print or audiobook sizes.
